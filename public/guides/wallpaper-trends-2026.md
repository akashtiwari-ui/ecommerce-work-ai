# Wallpaper Trends 2026: 7 Looks Defining Interiors This Year

> The biggest wallpaper trends of 2026: retro arches, moody botanicals, painterly gingham, terrazzo textures, ceiling wallpaper, dark luxe art deco and calming murals for kids.

## Key takeaways

- Retro 1970s arches and sunbursts remain the defining geometric trend of 2026.
- Moody botanicals in forest green replace bright tropicals.
- 'The fifth wall' — wallpapered ceilings — is the fastest-growing application.
- Painterly, hand-drawn textures beat crisp digital prints.

## 1. Retro arches and rainbows

Curved, 1970s-inspired motifs in mustard, rust and teal continue to dominate. They bring warmth and a sense of nostalgia, and they work in nurseries as well as living rooms.

## 2. Moody, enveloping botanicals

Deep forest grounds with layered ferns and gingko leaves create cocooning rooms, especially in studies and dining rooms.

## 3. Painterly gingham and checks

Soft-edged, watercolour checks bring a relaxed, cottage feel to kitchens — a softer take on 2024's crisp checkerboards.

## 4. Terrazzo and stone textures

Speckled terrazzo, travertine rings and pebble shapes add tactility to minimalist and Japandi spaces.

## 5. The fifth wall: ceilings

Wallpapering ceilings — especially in bedrooms and powder rooms — is a high-impact, low-area way to add pattern.

## 6. Dark luxe art deco

Gold-on-midnight fans and lattices turn small rooms into jewel boxes. Expect metallic inks and oxblood palettes.

## 7. Calming murals for kids

Low-contrast landscape murals designed for better sleep are replacing busy cartoon prints in nurseries.

## FAQ

### Is wallpaper still in style in 2026?

Yes. Wallpaper is more popular than at any time since the 1970s, driven by peel and stick formats, statement feature walls and ceiling wallpaper.

### What wallpaper colours are trending in 2026?

Forest green, terracotta, mustard, oxblood, sea-glass blue and warm neutrals like travertine and oat.
