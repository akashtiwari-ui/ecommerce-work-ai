# Can You Put Wallpaper in a Bathroom? A Practical Guide

> Yes — wallpaper works in bathrooms if you choose a scrubbable non-woven or vinyl finish, ventilate well and keep it away from direct water spray. Here is what to know.

## Key takeaways

- Wallpaper is safe in bathrooms with good ventilation and a scrubbable, mould-resistant finish.
- Powder rooms (no bath or shower) are the easiest and most impactful place for bold wallpaper.
- Never wallpaper inside a shower or directly behind taps and sinks — use tile or a glass splashback.
- Run an extractor fan for 15–20 minutes after showering.

## Which wallpaper is best for bathrooms?

Non-woven wallpaper with a scrubbable surface is the best choice for bathrooms. It's breathable, which helps moisture escape instead of getting trapped behind the paper, and it resists mould when used with a mould-inhibiting paste.

## Where can wallpaper go in a bathroom?

Safe zones are walls that don't get splashed: behind the toilet, above wainscoting or tile, and the walls of powder rooms. Avoid inside shower enclosures and directly behind basins without a splashback.

- Powder room walls — ideal
- Above tile or panelling — ideal
- Behind the toilet — good
- Next to a bath — only with good ventilation
- Inside the shower — never

## FAQ

### Will wallpaper peel in a bathroom?

Wallpaper can peel if the room has poor ventilation or the wrong paste is used. Use a mould-resistant paste, seal the seams and run a fan after bathing.

### Is peel and stick wallpaper waterproof?

Peel and stick vinyl is water-resistant and wipeable, but not waterproof. Steam and constant splashing can weaken the adhesive over time.
