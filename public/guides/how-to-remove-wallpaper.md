# How to Remove Wallpaper Without Damaging Walls

> How to remove peel and stick, non-woven and old paper wallpaper safely: peel at a low angle, strip dry, or score and steam stubborn paper. Step-by-step with tools.

## Key takeaways

- Peel and stick: lift a corner and peel slowly at a low angle, close to the wall.
- Non-woven: lift a bottom corner and strip each length off dry.
- Old paper-backed wallpaper: score, soak with warm water and fabric softener, or steam, then scrape.
- Wash off paste residue with warm soapy water before repainting.

## Removing peel and stick wallpaper

Start at a top corner and peel slowly, keeping the strip close to the wall at a low angle rather than pulling outward. If the adhesive resists, warm it with a hairdryer for 20 seconds.

## Removing non-woven wallpaper

Non-woven wallpaper is designed to strip dry. Lift a corner at the skirting board with a scraper and pull the length upward in one piece. Wash the wall afterwards to remove paste.

## Removing old, stubborn wallpaper

Score the surface with a scoring tool, soak with a mix of warm water and a little fabric softener, wait 15 minutes, then scrape. A steamer speeds up removal for multiple layers.

## How to remove wallpaper

Tools: Putty knife or scraper, Scoring tool, Spray bottle, Wallpaper steamer (optional), Sponge and bucket, Drop cloths

1. **Protect the room** — Lay drop cloths, switch off power to outlets and remove covers.
2. **Test a corner** — Lift a corner with a putty knife. If it peels off dry, simply strip it slowly by hand.
3. **Score and soak** — For stubborn paper, score the surface and spray with warm water mixed with fabric softener. Wait 15 minutes.
4. **Scrape** — Scrape the softened paper off at a low angle, taking care not to gouge the plaster.
5. **Wash the wall** — Wash off remaining paste with warm soapy water, let dry and fill any dents before repainting or re-papering.

## FAQ

### Does peel and stick wallpaper come off easily?

Yes, from smooth, cured painted walls it peels off in one piece without residue.

### Can you wallpaper over wallpaper?

It's not recommended. Removing the old layer gives better adhesion and a smoother finish.
