## FAQ

### How many rolls of Fern Cathedral do I need?

Measure wall width × height in feet to get square feet, then divide by 28 for peel & stick or 56 for non-woven, and add 10–15% for pattern matching and trimming. For example, a 12 ft × 8 ft wall (96 sq ft) needs 4 peel & stick rolls or 2 non-woven rolls. Use our free wallpaper calculator for an exact count.

### Is Fern Cathedral peel and stick or traditional wallpaper?

Both. Fern Cathedral is available as Peel & Stick ($72/roll, removable and renter-friendly) and as Non-Woven paste-the-wall wallpaper ($90/roll, the most durable, longer-lasting option). A $5 A4 sample is also available.

### What is the pattern repeat and match of Fern Cathedral?

Fern Cathedral has a 21" (53 cm) vertical pattern repeat and a half-drop match. With a half-drop match, every second strip is shifted down by half the repeat, so allow extra paper.

### Can I use Fern Cathedral in a bathroom?

It can be used in a well-ventilated powder room in the non-woven finish, but it is designed primarily for living room and bedroom and home office.
