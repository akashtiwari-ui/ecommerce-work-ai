## FAQ

### How many rolls of Harbour Gingham do I need?

Measure wall width × height in feet to get square feet, then divide by 28 for peel & stick or 56 for non-woven, and add 10–15% for pattern matching and trimming. For example, a 12 ft × 8 ft wall (96 sq ft) needs 4 peel & stick rolls or 2 non-woven rolls. Use our free wallpaper calculator for an exact count.

### Is Harbour Gingham peel and stick or traditional wallpaper?

Both. Harbour Gingham is available as Peel & Stick ($55/roll, removable and renter-friendly) and as Non-Woven paste-the-wall wallpaper ($69/roll, the most durable, longer-lasting option). A $5 A4 sample is also available.

### What is the pattern repeat and match of Harbour Gingham?

Harbour Gingham has a 6" (15 cm) vertical pattern repeat and a straight match. With a straight match, the pattern lines up horizontally across adjoining strips.

### Can I use Harbour Gingham in a bathroom?

Yes. Harbour Gingham is recommended for bathrooms in the non-woven finish, which is scrubbable and mould-resistant. Use it in ventilated rooms away from direct water spray.
