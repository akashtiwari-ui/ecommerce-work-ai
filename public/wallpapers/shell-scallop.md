### How many rolls of Shell Scallop do I need?

### What is the pattern repeat and match of Shell Scallop?

Shell Scallop has a 8" (20 cm) vertical pattern repeat and a straight match. With a straight match, the pattern lines up horizontally across adjoining strips.

### Can I use Shell Scallop in a bathroom?

Yes. Shell Scallop is recommended for bathrooms in the non-woven finish, which is scrubbable and mould-resistant. Use it in ventilated rooms away from direct water spray.
