## FAQ

### How many rolls of Milano Terrazzo do I need?

Measure wall width × height in feet to get square feet, then divide by 28 for peel & stick or 56 for non-woven, and add 10–15% for pattern matching and trimming (random-match patterns like this one need only about 5% extra). For example, a 12 ft × 8 ft wall (96 sq ft) needs 4 peel & stick rolls or 2 non-woven rolls. Use our free wallpaper calculator for an exact count.

### Is Milano Terrazzo peel and stick or traditional wallpaper?

Both. Milano Terrazzo is available as Peel & Stick ($59/roll, removable and renter-friendly) and as Non-Woven paste-the-wall wallpaper ($74/roll, the most durable, longer-lasting option). A $5 A4 sample is also available.

### What is the pattern repeat and match of Milano Terrazzo?

Milano Terrazzo has a Random match vertical pattern repeat and a random match. Random match means strips can be hung without aligning the pattern, so there is almost no waste.

### Can I use Milano Terrazzo in a bathroom?

Yes. Milano Terrazzo is recommended for bathrooms in the non-woven finish, which is scrubbable and mould-resistant. Use it in ventilated rooms away from direct water spray.
