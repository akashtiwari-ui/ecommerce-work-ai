## FAQ

### How many rolls of Boomerang Chevron do I need?

Measure wall width × height in feet to get square feet, then divide by 28 for peel & stick or 56 for non-woven, and add 10–15% for pattern matching and trimming. For example, a 12 ft × 8 ft wall (96 sq ft) needs 4 peel & stick rolls or 2 non-woven rolls. Use our free wallpaper calculator for an exact count.

### Is Boomerang Chevron peel and stick or traditional wallpaper?

Both. Boomerang Chevron is available as Peel & Stick ($49/roll, removable and renter-friendly) and as Non-Woven paste-the-wall wallpaper ($61/roll, the most durable, longer-lasting option). A $5 A4 sample is also available.

### What is the pattern repeat and match of Boomerang Chevron?

Boomerang Chevron has a 12" (30 cm) vertical pattern repeat and a straight match. With a straight match, the pattern lines up horizontally across adjoining strips.

### Can I use Boomerang Chevron in a bathroom?

It can be used in a well-ventilated powder room in the non-woven finish, but it is designed primarily for home office and kitchen and nursery & kids' room.
